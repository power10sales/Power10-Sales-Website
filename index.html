WHAT IS A POWER 10?

A Power 10 is a strategic rowing term. It is a call for all rowers to do ten of their best, most powerful strokes in order to pull ahead of the competition.

OUR PARTNERS

Winonics is a leading U.S. manufacturer of advanced rigid, flex, and rigid-flex printed circuit boards, specializing in quick-turn prototypes and high-mix production. With responsive engineering support, flexible manufacturing, and a commitment to quality and on-time delivery, Winonics helps customers move critical designs from concept to production with confidence. Partnered with Winonics International, we support everything from early prototype development through full mass production in a competitive global market.

Global Connector Technology Limited (GCT) is a leading supplier of standard and custom interconnect products. Our focus is on the supply of reliable high-quality connectors and cable assemblies. GCT leverages extensive worldwide resources to meet customer needs at local, regional and global levels. We have well-established sales, product development and manufacturing facilities in Europe, Asia and the Americas; GCT can support you wherever you design, wherever you manufacture.

METALfx was founded in 1976 as a precision sheet metal fabricator with a primary focus on networking equipment. Through the years, METALfx has diversified across many market sectors and now supplies vital parts and builds to industries such as food service, medical, semiconductor, and transportation. Our 125,000 square foot facility in Willits, CA, is where we focus on key performance metrics and offer extensive in-house capabilities.

Cut it, bend it, weld it, machine it, paint it, and assemble it. When it comes to precision sheet metal, METALfx will do it all or just what you need. Our experienced engineers help to improve customer designs for manufacturability and offer prototype to production support. Piece by piece our ISO-certified facility carefully builds and inspects each project to ensure conformance to customer requirements. From small precision parts to large cabinets, small quantities to high-volume, we have the experienced team, facility, and technology to turn your concept into reality.

King Epoxy Emblem (KTP) is a top manufacturer specializing in membrane switches, plastic injection molding, nameplates, in-mold decorative injection (IMD/IML), and metal stamping. With a factory in Thailand, KTP serves North America with a wide array of products and services, including in-house design, engineering, customer support, warehousing, and assembly. Our commitment to quality and innovation ensures top-tier solutions for projects of any size across various industries.
